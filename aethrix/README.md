A completely independent, original AI model developed from scratch with no third-party dependencies.

- Custom transformer-based architecture with novel attention mechanisms
- Trained entirely on our own curated datasets

- Transparency: Full control over architecture and training
- Security: Built with security-first design
- Accuracy: Trained with emphasis on factual reliability
- Continuous improvement: Self-improving capabilities
